Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Smartef Bookshop ("we", "us") operates this website. This page explains what information we collect and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide when registering: username, email address, and a security question answer (used only for password resets).
  • Purchase and payment records needed to unlock books you've bought.
  • Basic usage data collected automatically via Google Analytics (pages visited, approximate location, device/browser type). This is anonymized/aggregated and not tied to your account.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies to keep you signed in. If ads are shown on this site, our advertising partner (Google AdSense) may also use cookies to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

How we use your information

To operate your account, process payments, unlock purchased books, and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

We use Paystack to process payments and MongoDB Atlas to store site data. These providers have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data on our behalf.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the contact details on our site.
